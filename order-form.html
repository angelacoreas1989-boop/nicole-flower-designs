Order Policy

A 50% deposit is required to begin all custom orders. Deposits are applied toward the final order total.

Deposits are non-refundable if the order is cancelled after the 24-hour grace period.

Final payment must be paid in full no later than the day before delivery or pickup. Orders will not be delivered until payment is complete.

Rush orders may be accommodated when possible, but are not guaranteed. Please contact Nicole's Flower Designs before submitting this form if your order is needed quickly.

Orders over $250 may require a valid photo ID matching the name on the payment method.
