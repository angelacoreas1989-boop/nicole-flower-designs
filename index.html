Meet Norma

The Heart Behind the Designs

Norma is the floral designer behind Nicole's Flower Designs. With a love for flowers, creativity, and meaningful details, she handcrafts each arrangement with care, beauty, and attention to every special moment.

From fresh blooms to silk designs and custom wreaths, Norma creates floral pieces made to bring comfort, joy, and beauty to every home.

Our Story

A Family Legacy of Love

Nicole's Flower Designs began with family, remembrance, and a passion for creating beauty.

After the passing of her son, Tko, in 2008, Norma began creating floral arrangements as a way to honor his memory and express the love that remains.

Years later, her granddaughter Nicole encouraged her to share that gift with others and turn her creativity into a business.

Created in memory, crafted with love, and shared with others.
