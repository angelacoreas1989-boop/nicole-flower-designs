## ✦ LIVE WEBSITE ✦

### [Explore Nicole's Flower Designs](https://angelacoreas1989-boop.github.io/nicole-flower-designs/)

This project was created for a real family-owned floral design business using actual business requirements, services, branding preferences, ordering policies, and customer needs.

---

## ✦ PROJECT OVERVIEW ✦

Nicole's Flower Designs is a responsive small-business website created to give customers one central place to explore floral services, view previous work, understand ordering policies, and submit detailed custom quote requests.

The business specializes in:

- Custom fresh and silk arrangements
- Weddings
- Quinceañeras
- Sweet 16 celebrations
- Special events
- Sympathy and memorial arrangements
- Custom wreaths
- Local delivery
- Nationwide shipping

The project connects the business's social-media presence with a more structured online experience designed around the customer's decision-making process.

---

## ✦ THE BUSINESS PROBLEM ✦

Nicole's Flower Designs previously relied primarily on social media, direct messages, and word-of-mouth referrals.

Customers needed a centralized location where they could:

- Understand the services offered
- Browse examples of previous work
- Learn about delivery and shipping
- Review ordering and deposit policies
- Share information about an upcoming event
- Upload inspiration photographs
- Request personalized pricing
- Connect with the business through Instagram

Without a dedicated website, important details could require several separate conversations before the business had enough information to discuss pricing or recommendations.

---

## ✦ TARGET USERS ✦

### Primary User

A customer looking for a custom floral arrangement, wreath, wedding service, memorial tribute, or special-event design.

### User Needs

The customer needs to:

- Understand what the business creates

## ✦ THE SOLUTION ✦

I designed and developed a responsive floral-business website that serves as a professional online storefront and inquiry experience.

The website allows customers to:

- Learn about the business and its story
- Explore floral and event services
- Browse arrangement and wreath galleries
- Understand delivery and shipping availability
- Review ordering and deposit policies
- Complete a detailed quote-request form
- Upload inspiration photographs
- Connect through Instagram

The website helps the business collect more complete information before beginning a pricing and design conversation.

---

## ✦ CUSTOMER JOURNEY ✦

The primary customer journey follows these steps:

1. **Discover the business**  
   The customer learns what Nicole's Flower Designs offers.

2. **Explore services**  
   The customer reviews floral arrangements, weddings, quinceañeras, Sweet 16 celebrations, memorial designs, and wreaths.

3. **Review previous work**  
   Galleries help the customer understand the business's style and capabilities.

4. **Understand important policies**  
   The customer reviews delivery, shipping, deposit, and ordering information.

5. **Begin a quote request**  
   The customer selects an occasion, flower type, and estimated budget.

6. **Describe the vision**  
   The customer provides colors, preferences, event information, and additional details.

7. **Share inspiration**  
   The customer can upload photographs that communicate the desired style.

8. **Submit the request**  
   Formspree delivers the completed inquiry to the business for review and follow-up.

---

## ✦ DESIGN DECISIONS ✦

### Visual Identity

The website uses floral imagery, elegant typography, soft colors, and polished spacing to reflect the custom and luxury nature of the business.

### Services Before the Quote Form

Customers can review services and previous work before being asked to complete an inquiry. This provides context and helps them decide whether the business fits their needs.

### Detailed Quote Questions

Because custom floral pricing depends on occasion, flower type, size, design, budget, and timing, the form gathers important details in one structured workflow.

### Budget Selection

A budget field helps set expectations and gives the business useful information before recommending design options.

### Inspiration-Photo Upload

Customers can upload reference photographs because visual examples may communicate style preferences more clearly than text alone.

### Policy Visibility

Delivery, shipping, ordering, and deposit information is made available before submission so customers can understand important conditions early in the process.

### Mobile-Friendly Layout

Many customers reach small businesses through social media on mobile devices. The website was designed to remain usable on smaller screens.

### Direct Social Connection

Instagram integration allows customers to continue exploring the business's recent work and social-media presence.

## ✦ FORM AND PRIVACY INFORMATION ✦

The quote-request form uses Formspree to transmit customer inquiries to the business.

Customers should only provide information needed for a floral quote. The website should not be used to submit:

- Social Security numbers
- Banking information
- Credit-card details
- Government identification
- Medical records
- Passwords
- Other highly sensitive information

The form does not process payments or finalize orders. Pricing, availability, design details, deposits, and order confirmation are handled directly by the business after reviewing the request.
